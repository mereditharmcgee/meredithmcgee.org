**New structure:**
1. Single opening section (bg-dark bg-pattern) with `<h1>Hi, I'm Meredith</h1>` and no photo in the header area. Keep it simple: just the heading centered.
2. A single essay body section (bg-light) containing all the narrative content in flowing paragraphs inside a `.essay-body` container. Max-width: 800px, centered. Paragraph spacing: `margin-bottom: 1.75rem`.
3. Two photos placed inline within the essay as visual breaks (like a magazine article):
   - Headshot (`/meredith-headshot.jpg`) placed after the first 2-3 paragraphs, before the biographical arc. Use a centered `<figure>` with rounded corners and max-width ~350px.
   - Family photo (`/family-photo.jpg`) placed near the end, before or within the personal life paragraphs. Same centered figure treatment. Alt text: "Meredith with K and Ember".

**Content reshaping (use existing copy from about.astro, do NOT invent new content):**
The existing paragraphs contain all the content needed. Reshape them into a flowing essay with this arc:
- Opening: Who she is now, what drives her (from hero text + "What Guides" section). Tighten significantly. Use contractions throughout ("I'm", "I've", "it's", not "I am", "I have", "it is").
- Rochester upbringing (from "What Got Me Here" Rochester block)
- Lafayette: biology + history, Division I track, stepping back (from Lafayette block)
- Community work: OUT Lafayette, Lavender Lane, AIDS Quilt (from Lavender Lane block)
- Yale MPH: research, CBT trial, BRFSS data, evaluation (from Yale block)
- Bradbury-Sullivan: development, grants, $1M+ (from Bradbury-Sullivan block)
- Parabola Center: cannabis policy, qualitative study (from Parabola block)
- Personal life: K, Ember, theater, painting, nature, reading (from "What Brings Me Joy" section). Tighten "I am still drawn to many of the things" to use contractions and be more direct.
- Closing: brief forward-looking note (from "What Keeps Me Going" section, heavily condensed to 1-2 sentences)

**Cut entirely:**
- All 6 section headers (h2 tags)
- All institution logos and story-block layouts
- The story-conclusion italic paragraph
- The "What Keeps Me Going" section as a standalone section (condense into closing)
- The two-column hero layout
- The two-column joy layout

**Voice rules (check every paragraph):**
- Use contractions always ("I'm", "I've", "that's", "it's", "don't")
- No em dashes (use periods, commas, or restructure)
- No bullet points in prose
- No corporate buzzwords ("leverage", "utilize", "cross-disciplinary", "stakeholder")
- Lead with concrete, not thesis
- Vary sentence length

**CSS requirements:**
- `.essay-body` container: `max-width: 800px; margin: 0 auto; padding: 0 2rem;`
- `.essay-body p`: `margin-bottom: 1.75rem; font-size: clamp(0.95rem, 1.1vw, 1.05rem); line-height: 1.8;`
- `.essay-body p:last-child`: `margin-bottom: 0;`
- `.essay-photo`: centered figure with `max-width: 350px; margin: 2.5rem auto; border-radius: 12px; overflow: hidden; box-shadow: 0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.15);`
- `.essay-photo img`: `width: 100%; height: auto; display: block;`
- Responsive: at 768px, reduce photo max-width to 280px, reduce padding
- Section backgrounds: Use bg-dark bg-pattern for the heading section, bg-light for the essay body. Do NOT alternate sections. The essay is one continuous section.

**Page-specific styles go in a scoped `<style>` block. Use existing global utilities (.container, .bg-dark, .bg-light, .bg-pattern) from global.css. Do not duplicate them.**
